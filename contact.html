Frequently Asked Questions

What is the target ex-showroom pricing of the RT Series?

Target ex-showroom figures are: RT 120 (25 HP, 2.5 ft track) at ₹4.92 Lakh; RT 180 (38 HP, 3.0 ft track) at ₹6.70 Lakh; and RT 270 (52 HP, 3.5 ft track) at ₹9.25 Lakh. All figures include dealer margins and standard 2-year warranty provision.

How do track-width options benefit different crops?

Conventional tractors have wide fixed track widths that crush side roots in narrow vineyards, pomegranate orchards, or sugarcane rows. The RT Series provides 2.5 ft, 3.0 ft, and 3.5 ft track configurations, allowing the tractor to travel directly down narrow crop rows with zero root soil compaction.

Are Robotrac tractors eligible for government mechanization subsidies?

Yes. Eligible small and marginal farmers, women farmers, and SC/ST farmers can avail subsidies ranging between 40% and 50% under the Central SMAM scheme and Maharashtra MahaDBT agricultural mechanization portal. Our Paithan team assists with documentation.

What warranty and service support is provided?

Every RT Series tractor includes a 2-Year / 2000-Hour comprehensive factory warranty. Mobile Service Vans deployed from MIDC Paithan provide regular scheduled servicing directly on your farm field.

How can dealers and franchise partners apply?

Automobile and tractor dealership partners with dedicated showroom and service workshop space can apply using our Dealership Application tab above or email ceo@robotrac.co.in for territory allocation and margin details.
