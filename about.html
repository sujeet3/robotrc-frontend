Our Purpose

Smart Mechanization for Every Indian Farmer

Over 85% of Indian farmers cultivate small and marginal land holdings. Conventional 45–60 HP heavy tractors are often too wide for narrow fruit orchards, too heavy for delicate topsoils, and financially burdensome for small farm families.

Robotrac Tractor India Pvt Ltd was established with a singular mission: to engineer track-width optimized, autonomy-ready compact tractors and precision agricultural implements that drastically reduce input costs and increase farm profitability.

Track Widths

2.5 ft, 3.0 ft, and 3.5 ft track options for zero crop root damage.

Autonomy Core

Sensor pods & electronic drive-by-wire built into every tractor.

Strategic Roadmap

Milestones & Scalable Growth

From current Paithan operations to a 6,000 units capacity and planned public listing.

2024 – 2025

Platform R&D & Field Prototypes

Development and testing of the common modular smart platform with 2.5 ft, 3.0 ft, and 3.5 ft track widths across vineyard, sugarcane, and cotton fields in Maharashtra.

2026 (Current Milestone)

Production Launch at MIDC Paithan

Commercial roll-out of RT 120, RT 180, and RT 270 tractors alongside 16+ companion agricultural implements and electric multi-utility RT 270 EV concept.

2027 – 2029

National Dealer Expansion & 6,000 Units Plant

Scaling to a 6,000 units annual manufacturing capacity, pan-India dealership network, 70–80% domestic localization, and deployment of Level 2 field guidance kits.

2032 – 2033

Public Listing & Global Export Markets

Planned IPO trajectory and export of compact autonomous agricultural tractors to Southeast Asia, Africa, and Latin America.
